Clockfall Privacy Policy

EssenceAd ("we") explains here how the game Clockfall (Android app and web version, "the game") handles your information, in line with the Korean Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). The Korean version of this policy prevails if the two differ.

About the labels — sections marked Coming soon describe the cloud backup feature. It is not in the game yet and applies only from the day it ships. Even then it is off by default and only works if you turn it on in the settings and agree. If you don't turn it on, the game sends no personal information to any server.

Clockfall is a paid game (price announced at launch). Buy once for the full game, with no gacha, no currencies and no ads. New eras come as separate expansions. Purchases of the game and its expansions are processed by the app store (such as Google Play); we never receive card numbers or other payment details, and we handle no advertising IDs.

1. What we handle and why

1-1. Stored only on your device (not collected)

Your progress is stored only in your device's browser or app storage (IndexedDB / localStorage). It is never sent to a server, and we cannot see it.

  • Game save: character name (typed by you) and appearance, progress, shelter, storage, bag and gear, last location
  • Settings: volume, controls (drag aim, vibration), graphics quality, last loading screen

A file made with Export save in the settings stays on your device. Where you keep or send it is up to you.

1-2. Anonymous cloud backup Coming soon

We are preparing a cloud backup so you can continue after switching devices or reinstalling. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

  • It is off by default. It starts only when you tap Settings → Turn on cloud backup.
  • When you turn it on, a consent screen appears once. It shows what is collected, how long it is kept (2 years), how to delete it, and a link to this policy. Backup turns on only if you agree. If you don't, you can keep playing as usual.
  • The consent screen tells users under 14 to get a parent's or guardian's consent first, or not to turn it on (§6).
  • You can stop it any time with Settings → Turn off cloud backup. Once off, nothing more is sent. To also erase what was already uploaded, tap Delete cloud data.
  • The settings screen shows your anonymous ID. Quote it when you contact us.

1-3. Bug reports (only when you send them)

Settings → Bug report shows a text block with the items below. Copy puts it on your device's clipboard. The game never sends it on its own — we receive it only if you paste and send it yourself.

Line Contents
Build Game version label, build date, build number
Time Your device's current date, time and time-zone offset (e.g. +09:00)
Location In-game place name and coordinates
Status Character health, hunger and thirst values
Bag Slots used / total slots
Gear Tier and name of the weapons and armor you have equipped
Device Screen size and pixel ratio, OS type (Android, iOS, etc.), browser name and major version
Last 10 seconds Game log from the previous 10 seconds: console and error messages, on-screen notices, names of sound effects played
What happened Your own description
  • It does not include your character name, your full save file, advertising IDs or phone numbers.
  • Purpose: only to reproduce and fix bugs.
  • Retention: deleted after the fix, or within 1 year of receipt.
  • If you send it through a channel you choose (a tester group chat, email, etc.), that provider's privacy policy also applies.

1-4. What we do not collect

  • No ads. No advertising, analytics or automatic crash-reporting tools, and we do not read advertising IDs. Expansion purchases are handled by Google Play's billing system; we never receive payment details.
  • No access to location, contacts, photos/files, camera or microphone. The only Android permission used is vibration (plus internet access once cloud backup ships — used to send personal data only while backup is turned on).
  • No cookies.

2. Retention and deletion

  • On-device data (§1-1): removed when you uninstall the game or clear the site data in your browser. Uninstalling the app also deletes your on-device save.
  • Cloud backup (§1-2) Coming soon: deleted from the server without delay once you ask. Any backup copies held by the service provider are removed on its regular backup cycle.
  • Bug reports (§1-3): messages and emails are deleted after the retention period above.
  • Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not share your personal information with third parties, except where the law requires it (for example, a lawful request from investigative authorities).

6. Children under 14

The game does not ask your age and collects no personal information unless you turn cloud backup on. Cloud backup is off by default.

  • Children under 14 need consent from a parent or legal guardian to use cloud backup Coming soon.
  • The consent screen shown when turning backup on says: "If you are under 14, get your parent's or guardian's consent first, or don't turn this on." Please don't turn it on without that consent.
  • If we learn that data from a child under 14 was collected without a guardian's consent, we delete it right away.
  • Guardians can request access or deletion using §7, or delete it immediately with Settings → Delete cloud data on the child's device.

7. Your rights

You (or your guardian, if you are under 14) can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop processing your information.

  • On-device data: you can delete it yourself (uninstall the app, or clear the site data in your browser).
  • Cloud backup Coming soon: delete it right away in Settings → Delete cloud data. To just stop backing up, use Settings → Turn off cloud backup. If you have already removed the game, email cholybrown@gmail.com. Including the anonymous ID shown in the game settings helps us find it quickly.
  • We handle requests within 10 days and let you know the result.

8. Security measures Coming soon

  • All transfers are encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The database only lets each anonymous account read and write its own data.
  • Admin access is limited to the operator, and the admin account uses two-factor authentication.

This information site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These companies may process access logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies. We do not collect anything through this site.
